Prototype constraint

17–19 kHz is not guaranteed to survive platform transcoding, phone microphones, Bluetooth, or every speaker. This is intentionally a test harness—not a claim of production-grade watermarking. The marker is designed to identify the mix; track recognition can be added separately later.
